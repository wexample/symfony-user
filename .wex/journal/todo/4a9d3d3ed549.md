## 1. Split: symfony-user-ds holds the screens

Convention of the stack (`symfony-mail-ds`, `symfony-translations-ds`). A pure move, no behaviour change, suite green before and after.

- To `-ds`: `src/Controller/Pages/*`, `assets/pages/**`, the templates, scss, ts and `.trans.yml` of `assets/forms/**`.
- Stays in the core: entities, security (authenticators, handlers, gates, voters), form classes and their processors (they handle the ajax submits), services, mails (`assets/mails/**`), the journal.
- Route names out of the controllers into a core class (e.g. `Routing\UserRoute`): the core redirects to them (login, second factor, password, terms, authenticator setup). Without `-ds`, an application provides pages under those names; say it in `readme/installation`.
- Core tests that render a page (activation dead link) move or switch to a JSON/redirect assertion.
- Rewire: `symfony-user-demo`, the design-system app (MoJoe), syrtis, the doc manager container; temporary path installs until the owner publishes.
- Knowledge: split `readme/installation` and `usage/overview` between the two packages.

### Done (2026-10-03)

- `Routing\UserRoute` names every route; endpoints stay in the core (`SecurityEndpointController`, `PasswordLinkController`, `TwoFactorResendController`, `TotpBackupCodesController`), pages moved to `symfony-user-ds` (`Controller/Pages`), with `pages/`, `partials/`, `tunnels/`, `common/` and the templates, scss and ts of `forms/`.
- The `.trans.yml` of the forms stay in the core: a form reads its labels and errors from the domain of its class (`AbstractForm::transTypeDomain()`), not of its template.
- One behaviour changed: a dead activation link redirects to `/password/activation-invalid` instead of rendering at `/password/activate`.
- The core's fixture app loads `symfony-user-ds` (require-dev): the walks still reach real pages. Suite 117 green.
- `symfony-user-demo` requires `symfony-user-ds`.
- Left: two core services translate labels from the screens' domain (`ActivationProgressService`, `TotpSetupStepTrait` → `WexampleSymfonyUserDsBundle.pages…`); they could hand keys to the templates instead.
- Left to the owner: register `WexampleSymfonyUserDsBundle` in the applications (design-system app: not yet, its login page is gone until then); an application including `@WexampleSymfonyUserBundle/partials/user_menu.html.twig` (none found in PACKAGES — ai-editor?) moves to `@WexampleSymfonyUserDsBundle/...`.

## 2. Impersonation: the rules in the core

Symfony's `switch_user` already does the mechanics (`?_switch_user=`, `_exit`); the journal already has `account.impersonation_started` / `_ended`.

- A voter on the switch attribute (`ROLE_ALLOWED_TO_SWITCH`, the subject is the target account): refuses an account the actor does not administer (`AssignableRolesService`: `manages`, or the hierarchy), one an `AccountAdministrationGuardInterface` refuses, a disabled or locked one, oneself. Default on, no option to loosen it: impersonating a higher account is an escalation.
- `ImpersonationService::findTargets(actor, ?query, limit)`: the same voter filters every result, so the list never shows an account the URL would refuse, and the reverse.
- Listing mode decided server-side: at or under a threshold (option, e.g. 50 targets for this actor) everything is listed; above it, a search (2 characters minimum, limited results, rate-limited) — no enumeration.
- Each target shown with display name, email, roles: demo accounts are recognized without knowing their name.

### Done (2026-10-03)

- Who impersonates is the firewall's `switch_user` (absent → 404; under `when@dev` for Sapiens); who is impersonated is `impersonation.targets` (`administered` default, `any`), plus self / disabled / locked / application guards.
- Not a voter: with the affirmative strategy the role voter's grant wins. `ImpersonationGuardSubscriber` refuses on `SwitchUserEvent` instead, as `access.denied`.
- `require_intent` (default on): the switch needs the form's choice, kept in session 60 s — a bare link switches nobody.
- `ImpersonationService`: list up to `list_threshold`, search otherwise (2 chars, 20 results, 30/min). `GET /account/impersonate/search`.
- The fixture app needed `security.role_hierarchy` itself: without it Symfony registers no hierarchy voter, the parameter override alone is not enough.
- Tests: `ImpersonationTest` (7), `ImpersonationAnyTest` (1), each rule mutation-checked; suite 125 green. The page with its form cannot render in the fixture app (`form_load` needs the real layout): to check in an application.

## 3. The page, in symfony-user-ds

- A page with the select, posting through an ajax form with CSRF; the processor checks the voter again, then redirects to `?_switch_user=`. Never a bare GET link to switch: a link sent to an administrator would switch them.
- A JSON search endpoint for the remote mode.
- Protected at every layer, each refusing by default: `#[IsGranted]` on the controllers (configurable attribute, default `ROLE_ALLOWED_TO_SWITCH`), the application's `access_control` by path (documented), the voter on every result, the refusals journaled as `access.denied`.
- A "back to my account" action while impersonating (`IS_IMPERSONATOR`).
- Demo in `symfony-user-demo`: the demo accounts listed.

## 4. Searchable select: a generic input, not ours

No searchable select in the stack. Not `symfony/ux-autocomplete` (Stimulus and AssetMapper/Encore, foreign to our TS front). The impersonation page uses it; until it exists, a plain select for the listed mode.
